Raise a legion. Watch a thousand units clash.

Autobat Legions is a fantasy auto-battler. You hunt down units, level them up and grow their squads, then send armies of 1,000+ units against other players' armies. Every battle plays out on its own, so the fight is won before it starts: in who you recruit, how you upgrade them and where you put them.

Build your army

  • Collect 68 units (with more released over time) across five rarities, from goblin skirmishers and giant rats to ogres, liches, krakens and golden dragons.
  • Scout 10 regions (more unlock as the world opens up) for recruits. Each region has its own units, and climbing the ranks unlocks new ones.
  • Level up and grow your squads: a common squad can field over 150 units, while a legendary dragon fights almost alone.
  • Equip 100 trinkets that change how a squad fights: lifesteal, thorns, flight, extra units and more.
  • Arrange your lines: front-line tanks, a middle wave of flankers, and archers, casters and healers safe at the back.

Watch them fight

  • Huge, fully simulated battles on 20 battlefields with rivers, forests, bridges and lava.
  • Dozens of battle abilities: chain lightning, poison, freezing, charges, resurrection, splitting oozes, summoned spiders, tidal waves and avalanches.
  • Zoom in on the front line, or speed the battle up to 2x or 4x.

Ways to play

  • Ranked Arena: fight armies that other commanders saved at your rating and climb the Hall of Heroes leaderboard. Training bands fill in when nobody is close.
  • Challenges: 12 hand-made battles against named bosses across three chapters.
  • Legion Draft: an auto-chess party mode for 2 to 16 players (or bots). Everyone drafts an army from scratch in a shop, merges copies into stronger squads, and fights it out round by round.
  • Friend challenges: share an army code and fight each other's exact armies. Live head-to-head duels are in the downloadable version.

Play anywhere

  • Play in your browser right here, or download it for Windows, Linux or Android.
  • Sign in to keep your progress on the server and play from any device, or play offline as a guest.
  • Works with mouse and touch. On a phone, turn it sideways.
  • A short tutorial shows you the ropes.

Still in development

Autobat Legions gets regular balance patches and new content. Every change is listed in the in-game Patch Notes.
